# DEMO_CHECKLIST.md

## Recommended 8–10 Minute College Demo

### 1. Introduction — 30 seconds

Explain:

> The Smart Grocery Store Management System automates inventory, billing, sales tracking, and customer management.

### 2. Login — 30 seconds

Show authorized login.

### 3. Dashboard — 45 seconds

Show:

- Product count
- Low-stock count
- Today's sales
- Customers
- Recent transactions

### 4. Product & Inventory — 1.5 minutes

- Add a product.
- Show stock.
- Update stock.
- Show low-stock behavior.

### 5. Customer — 1 minute

- Add customer.
- Search customer.
- Show customer details.

### 6. POS — 2 minutes

- Search product.
- Add to cart.
- Change quantity.
- Show automatic total.
- Complete sale.

### 7. Inventory Effect — 30 seconds

Show that the sold quantity has been deducted.

### 8. Sales Report — 1 minute

Show the newly created transaction and updated total.

### 9. Error Handling — 45 seconds

Demonstrate one validation case, such as insufficient stock.

### 10. Future Scope — 30 seconds

Mention:

- AI-based product suggestions
- Mobile self-scanning
- Smart demand forecasting

## Backup Plan
